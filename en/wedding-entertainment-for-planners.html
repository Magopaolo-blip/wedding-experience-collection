Wedding entertainment for planners in Italy

Paolo Musetti works with planners and venues to fit live bubble performances and close-up magic into Italian wedding receptions. Share the schedule, guest count and venue layout to discuss the best format for your event.

Choose a format for the running order

  • Bubble Experience: a 20 or 30 minute show with a final giant bubble portrait of the couple.
  • Bubble Bar: an interactive station for cocktail hour, after dinner or a lounge.
  • Close-up magic: short performances for small groups or tables, coordinated with the catering team.

Coordinate with your venue

Share the proposed area, access for setup, available timings and the contact for catering, photography or production. For outdoor spaces, include the venue’s weather contingency. Setup and technical requirements depend on the format and the space.

Request availability and a proposal

Send the date, town and venue, guest count and the moment in the running order. Mention any technical sheet, travel, setup or insurance details your venue needs as part of the proposal.
