Practical notes

Frequently asked questions.

Where are the performances available?

Paolo Musetti is based in Rome and available for weddings and destination weddings throughout Italy.

When does the Bubble Experience work best?

The most effective placement is before the cake cutting or as a central performance, with timing coordinated with the event production.

Does the Bubble Experience require an indoor space?

Yes. An indoor environment provides the control required for precision, visual quality and artistic reliability.

Can the Bubble Bar be personalised?

Names, date and the wedding’s visual identity can be incorporated into the Bubble Menu and station details.

How can we check availability?

Send the date, venue, guest count and preferred moment by WhatsApp. Paolo will reply directly with the most suitable solution.
