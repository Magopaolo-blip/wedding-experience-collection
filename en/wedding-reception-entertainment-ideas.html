Wedding reception entertainment ideas

Start with the moment you want to shape. Cocktail hour calls for something that helps guests mingle. Dinner needs room for the catering team. Before the cake cutting, you can bring everyone together for a performance.

Cocktail hour: give guests a way to mingle

Roaming close-up magic brings short tricks to guests in small groups. A live Bubble Bar gives guests a place to gather, choose from the Bubble Menu and watch a creation take shape.

Dinner: work around the meal

Short visits to tables can be planned between courses. Share the seating plan and service timings with the performer, and protect the time reserved for speeches and hot dishes.

Before the cake: bring everyone together

A wedding bubble show creates a shared 20 or 30 minute performance, with a giant bubble portrait of the couple at the end. Arrange the timing and photographer’s position before the reception.

What to include when you enquire

Send your date, venue, guest count and the moment you want to enhance. If you are working with a professional, share the planning information for wedding planners.
